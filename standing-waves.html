Standing Waves

What are standing waves?

A standing wave is a stationary pattern formed when a wave and its reflection, travelling in opposite directions, interfere. Standing waves can form on stretched strings, pipes and in open or closed air columns. They contain fixed nodes, where displacement is zero, and antinodes, where displacement is greatest. Resonance occurs only at frequencies that fit an allowed number of half-wavelengths within the string or pipe, or an odd number of quarter-wavelengths in a pipe closed at one end. Each allowed pattern is called a harmonic.
