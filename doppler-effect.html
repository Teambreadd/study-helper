Doppler Effect

What is the Doppler effect?

The Doppler effect occurs when there is relative motion between a wave source and an observer, causing the observed frequency and wavelength to change. When the source moves towards the observer, the wavefronts are compressed, so the wavelength decreases and the observed frequency increases. When the source moves away, the wavefronts are spread out, so the wavelength increases and the observed frequency decreases.
